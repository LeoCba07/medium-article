What Is Ikigai, Really?

Ikigai (生きがい) is a Japanese concept that loosely translates to “reason for being.” It’s not about chasing passion or profit alone, but finding that sweet spot where what you love, what you’re good at, what the world needs, and what you can be paid for all meet. It’s often misunderstood as just a diagram — but in reality, it’s a lifelong practice of alignment, balance, and joy in everyday living.

The Four Circles That Shape Your Ikigai

At the heart of ikigai are four simple questions:

  • What do you love?
  • What are you good at?
  • What does the world need?
  • What can you be paid for?
These questions may look simple, but exploring them deeply can reveal surprising truths about who you are and what drives you. The overlap isn’t a fixed destination — it’s a compass that helps you navigate your career, relationships, and purpose.
